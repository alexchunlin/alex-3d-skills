# Evidence checklist

Each line names the evidence that settles it. A check row (`cadcheck.json` → `checks.<name>`) beats a `cad q` line,
which beats a render. Items marked with an ID caught real problems in that attempt. The ID points into `docs/FINDINGS.md`.

## Function
- [ ] Payload path: `drop` rows at every joint extreme **including the hard stops**, landing inside the working window (A1-F1), and `flattest_slide_deg` at least 5° above the stall angle (A3-F1: 21° at tilt 45 over a 20° limit). If no row exists, add a `drop_tests` entry and re-run the check rather than eyeballing.
- [ ] Brief numbers: a table of every numeric requirement in the brief against the design value. A deviation that the BOM doesn't call out is an issue (A3 R3 #6: nip range 5.5-10 against the brief's 9-12).
- [ ] Working condition reachable: `distances` **and `drop`** rows for the nip, gap or preload at both ends of the adjustment, and a modelled hard stop at each end (A1-F2; V2A1-F1: drop tested at the home hood setting only, stalls at the open end, the hood hit the body past the closed end). The drop "holds X at home" warn points at the missing rows.
- [ ] Indexing: one payload per cycle. `index` rows over the **documented** motion (BOM "how it works"), not the one you would choose: A2's 0 → 60 → 0 stroke released twice (A2-F2). Pocket size vs. payload in any orientation, **including two payloads side by side** (V2A1-F3: a Ø15 pocket took two edge-on 13 x 7 candies). Use `payload_fit` rows: a `pair_edge_on` row at the payload's **minimum** size must be blocked, a `flat` row at the **maximum** must fit, and `margin_mm` says by how much (V2A2-F2: a Ø14.4 pocket blocked a 13 x 7 pair by 0.11 mm and seated 12.5 x 6.5). Don't write a probe script: three agents did across v2 A1 and A2.
- [ ] Grip on the payload: the gap along the working line (`distances` with `ray`), the squeeze, and **which part yields** (A2-F4). A rigid interference on a hard payload is a major. Check the gap with the compliant part at **full deflection** too, at both adjuster ends: v2 A2's leaf ran out of travel below a 6.1 setting (V2A2-F1). Size the row for the **declared maximum** payload, from the params: `"min": "=MM_T[1] + TAPE_T"`. v2 A3 declared candies to 7.5 but its row asserted the nominal, and a 7.5 candy was rigidly squeezed over 46 % of the adjuster window (V2A3-F1). A typed-in number next to a declared range in params is a lead. **The gap at one station is not the passage:** where the payload travels an arc or a chute, the row's ray needs a `sweep` over the whole gripped arc (`cad rows cadcheck.json swept` prints the profile). v2 A4's four full-lift rows sat at BDC and passed at 8.28, while the hood, hinged far up the arc, left 7.50 at psi 80 (V2A4-F1). A params helper that asserts the gap (`gap_bdc`) over a few degrees is the same mistake. A ray that lands on another face than at home warns that it slipped past the working face.
- [ ] Joint sweeps: `interference` and `clearance` rows include overtravel. Margin past each limit, or a hard stop (A1-F5). A limit described as a hard stop needs a `distances` row at the limit showing the touching pair at about 0 (V2A2-F1: "the stud bottoms on the cover", but it was 2.36 clear). Every `fits` rule with `contact: true` on a pair that also has a journal needs `contact_normal`, or the journal is never measured (V2A1-V4).
- [ ] Rotating parts: retention on the shaft, balance, guard coverage (section through the wheel plane).
- [ ] Encoder or alignment: the gap and coaxiality numbers, and how the magnet or part is centred during assembly (A1-F7). Write the datum chain as a `stacks` entry and compare it to the sensor's limit (A2-F7).
- [ ] Cables: every brief cable has a route; `cables` rows show path vs. lead length; connectors and joints are not at a moving bend (A1-F12). Where a route ends at another route with no board or connector, the conductors run on: a `cable_chains` row sums them against the one lead you buy (V2A3-F3: a 150 mm encoder lead "continued in the bundle" for 430 mm). The junction warn lists those ends.
- [ ] Stability: `stability.worst` tip angle at the worst pose.
- [ ] Power, USB and cooling: plug clearance (`cad q dist`), and an air path past the heat sinks.

## Manufacture
- [ ] `parts`: all manifold, fit the P1S, no sloped overhang marked supports=no, bridges ≤ 25 mm, walls ≥ 1.6 mm.
- [ ] `fasteners`: no buried heads (reversed screws, A1-F10), engagement ≥ 4 mm, every bolt reaches through its nut (A2-F3), no shank or nut inside a purchased part (A2-F5), access at the assembly step and the tool it needs (straight bit or L-key arc, A1-F4, F11, A2-F1).
- [ ] Fasteners and cables included in interference (A1-F3). The `coverage` line must list all four collections.
- [ ] BOM fastener table vs the model: `cad bom BOM_AND_ASSEMBLY.md manifest.json` (size, length, head, count, stock lengths). V2R3 found an M3 x 20 stud that the model had as x 16, and an M3 x 9, by hand (V2A3-F5, F6).
- [ ] `assembly_order` matches the BOM steps, and every bench sub-assembly can actually be lifted into place. Read `assembly.step_of` in cadcheck.json before saying a part is in no step: V2R2 reported two parts as unplaced that were in steps 6e and 12 (V2A2-R1). The `assembly` warns list the parts really in no step and labels whose screw size disagrees with the model.
- [ ] Service: tape, shim, belt or battery changes without a full teardown. Count the screws (A1 R1 #6). Every BOM service claim has a `service` row, at the pose the BOM states (A2-F6). If the op removes a cover, a `slide_out` row has to show the cover comes off at that pose; cadcheck warns otherwise (V2A1-F2: the nip cover came off only at tilt 45). Service a driven part's own screws too: in V2A1-F4, the feeder servo swap needed a teardown that the BOM didn't mention.
- [ ] Purchased-part tolerances: `slide_out` rows; bays sized for the envelope plus 2 mm (A1-F13). A clearance to a toleranced purchased part has a `stacks` row with `"between"` (measured limit). A row whose `min` is far below its siblings on the same part is a lead: V2R5 found the detent tab at 0.322 from the jar band (row min 0.3, other band rows 2.0) while the band stack typed "designed clearance 3.0" (V2A5-F1). Read `summary.unbounded_allows`: an open `allow` across a joint hides any clash in that sweep (A5-06). "Removable" claims are swept over the **whole path**, including fasteners. A designer check at the rest pose only is an overclaim (A3-F3).
- [ ] Springs, flexures and snap arms: a `flexures` row each. The beam lies in the layer plane, or the stress stays under 15 MPa across layers (A3-F2).
- [ ] Sliding parts (gates, drawers, plungers) are `prismatic` joints, so the open state is in every sweep, and a detent riding one is a slide row `flex` bound, not an `ignore` (V2A3-T6: the reviewer moved the gate by hand in copies of the blend).

## Aesthetics
- [ ] iso and iso_front_left: no exposed wires, PCB edges or motor cans. `cables` exposure spots, nut-visible and fastener stand-off warns list the candidates with coordinates (A2-F8, F9, A3-F7, F8). Windows no bigger than what they must show.
- [ ] Edge breaks and radii from one declared set (grep the params for BEVEL/RADIUS).
- [ ] Visible screw heads counterbored; parting lines aligned.
- [ ] No leftover cut artefacts (notches, stray bosses) in hero views.
