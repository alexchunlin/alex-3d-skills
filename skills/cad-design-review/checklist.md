# Evidence checklist

Each line names the evidence that settles it. A check row (`cadcheck.json` → `checks.<name>`) beats a `cad q` line,
which beats a render. Items marked with an ID caught real problems in that attempt. The ID points into `docs/FINDINGS.md`.

## Function
- [ ] Payload path: `drop` rows at every joint extreme **including the hard stops**, landing inside the working window (A1-F1), and `flattest_slide_deg` at least 5° above the stall angle (A3-F1: 21° at tilt 45 over a 20° limit). If no row exists, add a `drop_tests` entry and re-run the check rather than eyeballing.
- [ ] Brief numbers: a table of every numeric requirement in the brief against the design value. A deviation that the BOM doesn't call out is an issue (A3 R3 #6: nip range 5.5-10 against the brief's 9-12).
- [ ] Working condition reachable: `distances` **and `drop`** rows for the nip, gap or preload at both ends of the adjustment, and a modelled hard stop at each end (A1-F2; V2A1-F1: drop tested at the home hood setting only, stalls at the open end, the hood hit the body past the closed end). The drop "holds X at home" warn points at the missing rows.
- [ ] Indexing: one payload per cycle. `index` rows over the **documented** motion (BOM "how it works"), not the one you would choose: A2's 0 → 60 → 0 stroke released twice (A2-F2). Pocket size vs. payload in any orientation, **including two payloads side by side** (V2A1-F3: a Ø15 pocket took two edge-on 13 x 7 candies). Use `payload_fit` rows: a `pair_edge_on` row at the payload's **minimum** size must be blocked, a `flat` row at the **maximum** must fit, and `margin_mm` says by how much (V2A2-F2: a Ø14.4 pocket blocked a 13 x 7 pair by 0.11 mm and seated 12.5 x 6.5). Don't write a probe script: three agents did across v2 A1 and A2.
- [ ] Grip on the payload: the gap along the working line (`distances` with `ray`), the squeeze, and **which part yields** (A2-F4). A rigid interference on a hard payload is a major. Check the gap with the compliant part at **full deflection** too, at both adjuster ends: v2 A2's leaf ran out of travel below a 6.1 setting (V2A2-F1). Size the row for the **declared maximum** payload, from the params: `"min": "=MM_T[1] + TAPE_T"`. v2 A3 declared candies to 7.5 but its row asserted the nominal, and a 7.5 candy was rigidly squeezed over 46 % of the adjuster window (V2A3-F1). A typed-in number next to a declared range in params is a lead. **The gap at one station is not the passage:** where the payload travels an arc or a chute, the row's ray needs a `sweep` over the whole gripped arc (`cad rows cadcheck.json swept` prints the profile; a missed station warns, V2A6-F3). v2 A4's four full-lift rows sat at BDC and passed at 8.28, while the hood, hinged far up the arc, left 7.50 at psi 80 (V2A4-F1). A params helper that asserts the gap (`gap_bdc`) over a few degrees is the same mistake. A ray that lands on another face than at home warns that it slipped past the working face.
- [ ] Joint sweeps: `interference` and `clearance` rows include overtravel. Margin past each limit, or a hard stop (A1-F5). A limit described as a hard stop needs a `distances` row at the limit showing the touching pair at about 0 (V2A2-F1: "the stud bottoms on the cover", but it was 2.36 clear). Every `fits` rule with `contact: true` on a pair that also has a journal needs `contact_normal`, or the journal is never measured (V2A1-V4).
- [ ] Rotating parts: retention on the shaft, balance, guard coverage (section through the wheel plane).
- [ ] Encoder or alignment: the gap and coaxiality numbers, and how the magnet or part is centred during assembly (A1-F7). Write the datum chain as a `stacks` entry and compare it to the sensor's limit (A2-F7).
- [ ] Cables: every brief cable has a route; `cables` rows show path vs. lead length; connectors and joints are not at a moving bend (A1-F12). Where a route ends at another route with no board or connector, the conductors run on: a `cable_chains` row sums them against the one lead you buy (V2A3-F3: a 150 mm encoder lead "continued in the bundle" for 430 mm). The junction warn lists those ends. A route that ends on a part, or meets another route, on another body has to meet on the joint axis: `cables.end_drift` gives how far the ends come apart at each limit (V2A6-F2: the static pan lead 8.8 mm from the off-axis splice at pan -90; the base/bundle `distances` row passed only because the sleeves overlap). `cables.kinks` and `cables.shared_runs` list corners tighter than 0.75 x OD (a turn right at a plug reads radius 0) and two routes in one tube (V2A7-F2: both bus leads in one tube onto the splice, turning 90 deg 1.5 above its face).
- [ ] Stability: `stability.worst` tip angle at the worst pose.
- [ ] Power, USB and cooling: plug clearance (`cad q dist`), and an air path past the heat sinks.

## Manufacture
- [ ] `parts`: all manifold, fit the P1S, no sloped overhang marked supports=no, bridges ≤ 25 mm, walls ≥ 1.6 mm.
- [ ] **Printability is re-run on the exported STLs, not taken from the hand-off:** `cad printcheck <project>/stl --manifest <project>/blender/manifest.json --render <review_dir>/printcheck` (and `cad slice <project>/stl` if `cad slice --detect` shows a usable slicer). Every printcheck fail (island, cantilever, slope under 30°, tangent bed contact) on a part marked supports: no is a blocker when the brief forbids supports; open the layer map PNG before you write it up. v2 shipped "no supports" on every part and four needed them (jar_valve tabs, feeder_cover ears, base_shell tie block, post_R top; FINDINGS "v2 post-freeze"). Never run a slicer any other way.
- [ ] `fasteners`: no buried heads (reversed screws, A1-F10), engagement ≥ 4 mm, every bolt reaches through its nut (A2-F3), no shank or nut inside a purchased part (A2-F5), access at the assembly step and the tool it needs (straight bit or L-key arc, A1-F4, F11, A2-F1).
- [ ] Fasteners and cables included in interference (A1-F3). The `coverage` line must list all four collections.
- [ ] BOM fastener table vs the model: `cad bom BOM_AND_ASSEMBLY.md manifest.json` (size, length, head, count, stock lengths). V2R3 found an M3 x 20 stud that the model had as x 16, and an M3 x 9, by hand (V2A3-F5, F6).
- [ ] `assembly_order` matches the BOM steps, and every bench sub-assembly can actually be lifted into place. The `assembly` warns list the parts really in no step and labels whose screw size disagrees with the model. Read `checks.insertion`: a part or merged group with no straight way in, and a bench part far from its group, are listed with their blockers. A BUY part the BOM places "in the bore" at a late step is the case to look for (V2A6-F1: the bus splice in the feeder's bench step, under a groove cap fitted at 2c). Read `assembly.held_poses`: a step that moves a servo-held joint (its `pose`, or a screw reachable only at a joint limit) before any step is `powered` means back-driving the servo by hand, unless the step's `pose_how` says how (V2A7-F1: the groove cap at 11a needed tilt 45, the tilt servo in since step 8, the battery at 14). A removal row that stops short of clearing the machine proves nothing; extend it to infinity or until the part is clear (V2R7 A7-03).
- [ ] Service: tape, shim, belt or battery changes without a full teardown. Count the screws (A1 R1 #6). Every BOM service claim has a `service` row, at the pose the BOM states (A2-F6). If the op removes a cover, a `slide_out` row has to show the cover comes off at that pose; cadcheck warns otherwise (V2A1-F2: the nip cover came off only at tilt 45). Service a driven part's own screws too: in V2A1-F4, the feeder servo swap needed a teardown that the BOM didn't mention.
- [ ] Purchased parts: every BUY part reaches its seat by a `slide_out` row along the stated path, with the parts fitted later in `ignore` and the row's `min_clearance` not lowered below 0.2 on a printed seat (PA-A8-T7: rows set 0.0 and passed at 0.0). A part `allow`ed against the mover drops out of the row: narrow the allow (PA-A8-T1); `flex` is for springs (PA-A7-T14, T19: a slot 0.2 mm short passed every check for two rounds). Bays sized for the envelope plus 2 mm (A1-F13). A clearance to a toleranced purchased part has a `stacks` row with `"between"` (measured limit). A row whose `min` is far below its siblings on the same part is a lead: V2R5 found the detent tab at 0.322 from the jar band (row min 0.3, other band rows 2.0) while the band stack typed "designed clearance 3.0" (V2A5-F1). Read `summary.unbounded_allows`: an open `allow` across a joint hides any clash in that sweep (A5-06). "Removable" claims are swept over the **whole path**, including fasteners. A designer check at the rest pose only is an overclaim (A3-F3).
- [ ] Load-path parts have a print spec (walls, infill, material) in the BOM, and stress rows use the printed section, not a solid one (PA-A8-T13: 133 MPa at the piston mouth at slicer defaults).
- [ ] Springs, flexures and snap arms: a `flexures` row each. The beam lies in the layer plane, or the stress stays under 15 MPa across layers (A3-F2).
- [ ] Sliding parts (gates, drawers, plungers) are `prismatic` joints, so the open state is in every sweep, and a detent riding one is a slide row `flex` bound, not an `ignore` (V2A3-T6: the reviewer moved the gate by hand in copies of the blend).

## Aesthetics
- [ ] iso and iso_front_left: no exposed wires, PCB edges or motor cans. `cables` exposure spots, nut-visible and fastener stand-off warns list the candidates with coordinates (A2-F8, F9, A3-F7, F8). Windows no bigger than what they must show, plus 2 mm (A3-F8).
- [ ] Edge breaks and radii from one declared set (grep `params.py` for `EB`/`CR`, as in the project template). A `parts` warn "overlap clamp cut the edge break" means the part's breaks are a fraction of the declared size (V2A6-T2).
- [ ] Visible screw heads counterbored; parting lines aligned.
- [ ] No leftover cut artefacts (notches, stray bosses) in hero views.
