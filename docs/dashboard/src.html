Skills and tools that let Claude agents design 3D-printable assemblies in headless Blender, then check them like a reviewer would: joint sweeps, fastener access, insertion order, cable bends, print supports. Every rule traces back to a finding from a real design loop. Two projects ran through it: the M&M launcher (v1 with no tools, v2 with them) and a chair push-assist unit.

Known gaps

  • A slide_out removal row that stops short of clear still passes (V2R7 A7-03).
  • Insertion is straight-line only. Parts made in place, like a soldered splice, need insert_ok.
  • Rows whose limit follows the geometry pass by construction (V2A5-F1).
  • cad slice's G-code parsing has only met stand-in output; no real slicer is installed.
  • Creep and fatigue are out of scope. Gear tooth tips read as thin walls.

What's in the repo

  • print-cad-blender: cadlib (geometry, print features, assembly, export), cadcheck, cadq and the cad wrapper.
  • cad-design-review: the reviewer's check-first protocol and scoring anchors.
  • cad-loop.workflow.js: plan, parallel builders, finish, review. Resumable, model per role.
  • verify_all.sh: re-proves every claim against 10 archived designs in about 28 min.
  • tools/bench: replay recall and noise; cc_transcript.py: agent time and token cost.
